What to test — 0.9.0

Your last build was 0.7.0. This one is the third recorded review, and most of it
lands on the desktop — but three things here are worth opening the phone for.

The chat view now works for a session running on another computer. Open a
session that lives on your PC and switch it to chat: the transcript is read on
the machine that owns it and drawn here, same bubbles, same composer.

The account a session is running under is named rather than called "Default".
If a session on a paired machine used to show that word, it should now show the
login it is actually on.

Sessions are called by their own names. Every session started in the copilot's
folder used to be labelled "Commander", including ones you had renamed.

Everything else is desktop-side: the app's type is one notch larger, the copilot
side panel is a chat instead of a feed, downloads work and can be pointed at
another machine, and a browser opened by a session on another computer no longer
opens on the Mac you happen to be sitting at.
