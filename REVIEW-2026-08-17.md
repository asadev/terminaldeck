# Part 4 — driving mode, rebuilt around a different idea

**This inverts what was built, and the inversion is the whole point.**

Driving mode today stops at each place *for him to read*, paced to reading
speed. He does not want that:

> *"Currently it stays for us to read. Let's not make it for us to read. It will
> do it in a way that it is reading it in very fast mode — it is going here and
> there, it is going to all of those sessions… and we can see which words it is
> making focus… it is scanning everything very fast and we can see like a machine
> is working, a proper feel of high-speed intelligence."*

So there are two phases, and the reading is at the end, not during:

1. **The scan** — machine speed, visibly. It moves through the sessions, focuses
   on words, and he *watches* rather than reads. No pacing to human reading
   speed, no pause-per-stop.
2. **The answer** — it returns to its own chat and **combines everything into one
   structured response**: *"this session did this, this session did that."* Then
   it stops, and he reads at his own pace, in one place.

**Consequence for what exists:** `estimate.ts` (reading time from word count and
density) and the pacing controls were built for phase 1 as a paced read-along.
That purpose is gone. The interruption handling and the transport still matter,
but the model of "hold each stop long enough to read it" does not.

## Scope of the scan

- **Every session, not only its own.** Including ones he started himself.

## The look of it

- An **intelligence UI** — *"some dots around it or kind of things happening,
  interactive UI, maybe WebGL"* — conveying scanning at speed.
- **Non-focused areas carry the dots treatment**; the focus is what stays clear.

## Scraping behaves the same way

- Asked to scrape, it goes, **shows the page and how it is scraping**, then
  returns with the result.

## The toggle — both modes are required

- **Interactive mode ON** — the visible scan above.
- **Interactive mode OFF** — it does the work in the background and returns the
  final answer normally, with none of the driving.

## Where it lives while driving

- The small round **dot beside the copilot's name becomes a control** — click it
  to fold back, and the side panel returns.
- While driving, **the side panel is the copilot**: chat box there, continuously
  talking, and he can drive to other pages and bring it back.

## The layout rule — stated twice, so it matters

> *"When it is interacting it is making two split views even inside its own page.
> It should not make two split views on its own page."*

- **On its own page:** no split. It stays as it is. **The side panel is not its
  area.**
- **On any other page:** it takes the side panel, and only then.

## It must know where he is

  answer — it should know which page I am on, what I am looking at."*
- While it is on a page, he can **see what is in that browser tab** and talk
